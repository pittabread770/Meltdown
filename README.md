# Meltdown

A small item mod focused on nuclear-themed and synergistic items and effects. Now with Red Alert support!

## Items
| Icon | Name | Effect |
| - | - | - |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/YvdnRh1Q/tex-Icon-Pickup-Plutonium-Rounds.png) | Plutonium Rounds | Activating a non-primary skill damages 1 (+1 per stack) enemies in a 30m (+5m per stack) radius around you for 200% base damage, irradiating them. |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/Rhxq7sxm/tex-Icon-Pickup-Busted-Vent.png) | Busted Vent | When activating your utility skill, or when it comes off cooldown, damage enemies in a 12m (+4m per stack) radius around you for 150% base damage. Additionally, enemies are irradiated for 6s (+3s per stack). |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/90Hzd8Hc/tex-Icon-Pickup-Thermite-In-ACan2.png) | Thermite-in-a-Can | Increase your minions damage by 10% (+10% per stack) and chance on-hit to ignite by 10% (+10% per stack). |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/vTwDrqws/tex-Icon-Pickup-Old-Exhaust-Pipe2.png) | Old Exhaust Pipe | Activating your utility skill increases your movement speed by 25% (+25% per stack) for 6s. |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/Wd5W8hMw/tex-Icon-Pickup-Uranium-Fuel-Rods.png) | Uranium Fuel Rods | Gain a 5% chance to irradiate enemies on hit. Your Irradiated effects deal 200% (+200% per stack) increased damage, and last 50% (+50% per stack) longer. |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/nX3R2CvR/tex-Icon-Pickup-Leaky-Reactor-Coolant.png) | Leaky Reactor Coolant | Gain a 5% chance to irradiate enemies on hit. Irradiated enemies lose 15 (+15 per stack) armour, and have a 20% (+20% per stack) chance per tick to ignite. |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/XrsHLpwg/tex-Icon-Pickup-Volatile-Thorium-Battery.png) | Volatile Thorium Battery | Gain a 5% chance to Irradiate enemies on hit. Irradiated enemies have a 10% chance per tick to deal 200% damage in a 12m (+4m per stack) radius, applying Irradiated. |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/9DL8brGx/tex-Icon-Pickup-Charcoal.png) | Charcoal | Gain a 5% chance to burn enemies on hit. Burning enemies have a 5% chance per tick to scorch 2 (+2 per stack) nearby enemies in a 20m (+5m per stack) radius. |
| ![](https://i.postimg.cc/fV82CJx8/tex-Icon-Pickup-Abandonment.png) | Abandonment | Decrease secondary skill cooldown by 50% (+50% per stack). Decrease primary skill damage by 25% (+25% per stack). |
